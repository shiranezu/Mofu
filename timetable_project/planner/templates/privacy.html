Privacy Policy.

Last updated June 2026

What we collect

To run your account, Mofu stores the information you give it directly: your name, username, email address, and password (encrypted, never stored in plain text).

Everything you create inside the app — timetables, tasks, notes, study sessions, goals, resources, and schedule entries — is stored so the app can show it back to you. We don't collect anything beyond what's needed to make the app work.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data to anyone, ever.
  • We don't show ads or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't read your notes or study content for any purpose other than displaying it to you.

How your data is stored

Your data is stored in a secure database and is only accessible through your logged-in account. Passwords are hashed and never visible to anyone, including us.

Your data, your control

You can export a full copy of your data at any time from Settings → Data Export. You can also permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Danger Zone — this action cannot be undone.

Cookies

Mofu uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. That's it — no tracking cookies, no third-party analytics scripts.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated. Continued use of Mofu after changes means you accept the updated policy.

Questions?

Reach out through the Contact link in Help & Support.
