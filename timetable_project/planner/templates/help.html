Help & Support.

Everything you need to get the most out of Mofu.

Planning
Timetables · Schedule · Tasks
Timetables
Build your weekly class schedule. Add timeslots, assign subjects and locations, and colour-code by category.
Schedule
A dynamic calendar for your day-to-day entries. Pick a date, add an entry, and tick it off when done.
Tasks
Organise your to-dos into boards. Set priorities, due dates, and track progress as you complete each one.
Focus
Timer · Notes · Dictionary · Resources
Timer & Pomodoro
Track study sessions with a regular timer, countdown, or the Pomodoro technique. Sessions are saved automatically.
Notes
Write and save notes with rich text. Organise them by category and access them from anywhere in the app.
Dictionary & Resources
Look up word definitions on the fly, and save useful links and references to your resource library.
Goals & Growth
Categories · Goals · Streaks
Categories
Create colour-coded categories to organise sessions, notes, and resources by subject or topic.
Goals
Set daily or weekly study hour targets. Mofu tracks your progress and shows how close you are to hitting them.
Streaks
Stay consistent. Mofu tracks how many days in a row you've studied and celebrates your progress.
Insights
History · Statistics · FAQs
History
Browse all past study sessions. Add notes, rate productivity, and review what you worked on.
Statistics
See charts and breakdowns of your study time — by day, week, and category — to understand your habits.
FAQs
Common questions answered — from how sessions are recorded to how streaks are calculated.
